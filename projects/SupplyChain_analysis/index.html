Supply Chain Analytics

Problem: A business was struggling with shipping delays but couldn't identify the root causes, impacting customer satisfaction and operational costs. Action: I conducted an in-depth Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) in Python and developed an interactive Tableau dashboard to visualize the entire logistics pipeline. Result: The analysis clearly pinpointed that 'Standard Class' shipping was the primary source of delays, allowing the company to focus on improving that specific service level.

Key Business Questions

This analysis aimed to answer critical questions for optimizing the supply chain:

Which shipping modes have the longest delays?

Which products are most profitable?

Which regions experience the most delays?

How do shipping costs vary by product category?

Tableau Dashboard

A two-page Tableau dashboard was created to provide a comprehensive view of the supply chain. The first page focuses on logistics and shipping performance, while the second explores customer and product profitability.

Key Insights

Standard Class Delays

The 'Standard Class' shipping option is the single largest contributor to delivery delays, indicating a need for process optimization.

Category Profitability

Technology products, despite having high shipping costs, are the most profitable category due to high margins.

Customer Segmentation

Corporate clients are the most valuable segment, consistently placing high-volume, profitable orders.

Regional Bottlenecks

The dashboard revealed specific warehouse-to-delivery routes that consistently underperform, highlighting logistical weak points.

Process & Analysis

The project involved connecting to the dataset, performing extensive data cleaning and feature engineering in Python (Pandas), and then creating calculated fields in Tableau. A key SQL-style analysis in Pandas involved grouping by shipping mode to find the average time to ship, confirming the 'Standard Class' issue.

Conclusion

By leveraging Python for deep data analysis and Tableau for intuitive visualization, this project successfully translated complex supply chain data into a clear and actionable tool. The findings directly empower stakeholders to address inefficiencies, optimize shipping strategies, and ultimately improve customer satisfaction and profitability.
